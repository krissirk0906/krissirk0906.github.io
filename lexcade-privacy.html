🔒 Privacy Policy

Lexcade — Premium Word Puzzle Game

Effective Date: May 10, 2026
Last Updated: May 10, 2026

Lexcade ("the App") is developed by June Nine ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices regarding your data when you use the App.

📋Information We Collect

Information you provide directly:

  • Display name — entered during onboarding, stored locally on your device
  • Game statistics — wins, losses, streaks, guess distribution (stored locally via Hive)
  • Settings/preferences — hard mode, theme, sound, notification preferences (stored locally)

Information collected automatically:

  • Leaderboard data — if you participate in the daily puzzle, your display name, score, puzzle day, and solve time are submitted to our Supabase backend for the leaderboard feature
  • Device-generated player ID — a random identifier created on your device to associate leaderboard entries (not tied to your real identity)

We do NOT collect:

  • Email address, phone number, or real name
  • Precise location or GPS data
  • Contacts, photos, or files from your device
  • Browsing history or cookies
  • Advertising identifiers

🎯How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and maintain the game experience
  • Display your game statistics and progress
  • Show your rank on the daily puzzle leaderboard
  • Send optional daily reminder notifications (you can disable this)
  • Process in-app purchases (premium unlock) via Google Play

🔗Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Supabase — backend for leaderboard functionality. Stores your player ID, display name, scores, and solve times.
  • Google Play Services — handles in-app purchases and app distribution. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

We do not use analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or tracking tools.

💾Data Storage & Security

Game data is stored locally on your device using Hive (local database). Leaderboard data is transmitted to Supabase over encrypted HTTPS connections. No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we use commercially acceptable means to protect your data.

🗑️Data Retention & Deletion

  • Local data: remains on your device until you uninstall the App or clear its data
  • Leaderboard data: retained on our Supabase backend to maintain leaderboard integrity
  • To request deletion of your leaderboard data, contact us at the email below with your player ID

👶Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will promptly delete such data.

🔔Notifications

The App may send daily puzzle reminder notifications. You can disable notifications at any time through the App's Settings screen or your device's system settings. Notifications are scheduled locally and do not involve external servers.

💳In-App Purchases

Lexcade offers an optional one-time premium purchase ($2.99) to unlock all features. Payment processing is handled entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card details, billing address, or financial information.

🌍International Users

Your data may be processed in countries where Supabase operates its servers. By using the App, you consent to the transfer of your information as described in this policy.

📝Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. You are advised to review this page periodically.
